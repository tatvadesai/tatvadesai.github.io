the desk turns company valuation into a live team decision. teams study public financials and a private clue, submit a number, then choose whether to trade against the market price made from the room's valuations.

i designed the game mechanics, player and organiser screens, room display, and rehearsal process. the work moved from a browser prototype to a live event system, then changed through simulation and rehearsal. the case study covers the decisions behind that experience.
