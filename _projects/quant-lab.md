the six are a fama-french factor lab, options analytics (black-scholes, the greeks, monte carlo), fundamental research (dcf, capm and wacc, set up for indian companies), sentiment analysis of earnings calls with the loughran-mcdonald dictionary, a backtester for well-known strategies, and a pulse of alternative data on india.

they share one written standard for structure, tests and documentation, with 58 tests between them.
