ekagrit ventures is where i think out loud about markets, companies and questions i can't answer yet. notes start life in obsidian, the same vault i use every day, and publish without any copy and paste.

two small jekyll plugins make that work: one turns note titles into clean urls, the other turns obsidian's wikilinks into real links. a github action builds and deploys on every push. it is past 144 commits so far.
