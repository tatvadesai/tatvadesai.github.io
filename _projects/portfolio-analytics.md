prices come straight from yahoo with no api keys. from there it reports sharpe, sortino and calmar ratios, var and cvar, beta and alpha, runs stress scenarios, and draws the efficient frontier with the max-sharpe and minimum-volatility portfolios marked.

everything ends in an automatically written report, so a run produces something you could hand to someone, not just a notebook.
