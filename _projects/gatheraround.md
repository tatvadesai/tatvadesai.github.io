gatheraround puts interesting strangers around one table. you sign up, we handle the venue, the menu and the people, and you just show up. it ran ten meetups in its first three months.

the software is two halves. the public side is the site and waitlist. the private side is an admin dashboard that runs the business: events, bookings, a upi payments ledger, expenses and the financials, all on supabase with row-level security.

running it also produced side tools, like a bulk messenger that sends personalised whatsapp invites from a spreadsheet.
