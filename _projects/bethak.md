if you grew up around gujarati elders, you have heard hundreds of these at weddings and in kitchens, usually halfway through an argument. very few of them are written down anywhere you can search.

bethak collects them from four public-domain books, ocr'd page by page on free-tier gemini, plus wikiquote. every saying keeps its source, and sayings that appear in more than one edition are marked as corroborated, so you can tell a well-attested proverb from a one-off.
