Selected work

Four systems. Four decisions.

Showing the full portfolio bank.

Decision

Compare base and stress loss, locate concentration, then trace each signal to its triggering evidence.

Method

Python, DuckDB SQL, Streamlit, deterministic EWI rules, EAD-weighted PD, expected loss and HHI.

Evidence

Six generated obligors, $420m assumed EAD, two base warnings and three under stress.

Boundary

No real obligors, RWA, economic capital, approvals or production workflow.

Immutable snapshots, SHA-256 manifests, atomic mart replacement and read-only dashboard access keep the analytical path reviewable.

Source & tests — wholesale-credit-monitor · Live app (Streamlit)

Decision

Translate an estimated retail PD into an interpretable points score and a bounded expected-loss illustration.

Method

WoE/IV screening, logistic regression, 50-PDO scaling, AUC, Gini, KS, PSI and calibration.

Evidence

1,000 UCI records; test Gini 0.584, KS 0.498 and train/test PSI 0.034.

Boundary

Retail application scorecard. Not wholesale, Basel, IFRS 9 or approved lending infrastructure.

The model card separates fitted PD from assumed LGD and EAD, and lists the controls required before any real use.

Source & validation — credit-risk-engine

Decision

Separate the investor's money-weighted experience from the portfolio's time-weighted performance, so each question is answered by the measure built for it.

Method

FIFO lot accounting over broker exports, then XIRR, TTWR, Modified Dietz, CAGR and rolling 63/126/252-session windows against the NIFTY 50.

Evidence

Per-holding price sparklines, three rolling windows on one shared scale, a monthly return grid and drawdown against the index.

Boundary

The shipped sample is fictional. Dividends, corporate actions, taxes and brokerage are not applied. Not a tax document or investment advice.

The report opens on the distance between XIRR and TTWR and names it as the cost of cash-flow timing, because the two measures answer different questions and disagreeing is what they are for.

Source & report — portfolio-tearsheet

Decision

Move from a central forecast to a P50 plan and empirically calibrated P80 order-up-to level.

Method

R, fixed 52-week holdout, harmonic regression, seasonal-naive benchmark and residual calibration.

Evidence

1,463 fictional weekly observations; baseline MASE 1.116 and P80 coverage 80.8%.

Boundary

Public figures are synthetic. Private Extra Trees, allocation and business outcomes remain unverified.

The strongest modelling decision was restraint: keep complexity only when it improves the operating decision.

Source & case study — demand-forecasting-synthetic

Working principles

Make the assumption visible. Keep the baseline. Show what failed. End with the decision.
